01 / The idea

Real voices sell better.

Young audiences trust people, not brands. Instead of relying only on brand content, I designed a way for participants to tell their own story, with guidance from me, freedom in their own voice, and a real reason to keep creating.

02 / My role

I designed the whole system.

03 / How it works
Step 1

During the program

I brief participants on what makes good content and the opportunities to grow, so they create from day one.

Step 2

Selection

After each program, I identify the most outstanding creators and invite them to join Voices.

Step 3

Voices

Ambassadors share their experience, programs and scholarships, competing for a full scholarship to come back.

Step 4

Enablement

Masterclasses, clear guidelines that leave room for their own voice, and a usage agreement to reuse the content.

From the Voices onboarding I designed for each new cohort.
