01 / The challenge

A brand without a voice.

When I joined, TrepCamp had no defined audience, no consistent tone and no clear visual identity. Content was published for the sake of posting, without answering the basic questions: who are we talking to, and what do we want them to feel, think or do?

03 / What I did

Audience & positioning

Tone of voice

3
